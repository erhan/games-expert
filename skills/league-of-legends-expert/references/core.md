# League of Legends core knowledge

- Main contexts: Summoner's Rift roles, ARAM, Arena or rotating modes, and competitive play.
- Champion advice should cover identity, trading pattern, power spikes, wave plan, matchup risks, teamfight role, and item/rune adaptation.
- Improvement advice should use a small measurable focus such as CS, deaths before objectives, recall timing, vision, or reviewing repeated mistakes.
- Champion numbers, items, runes, objectives, matchmaking systems, and tier lists are patch-sensitive.
- Current-source starting points: https://www.leagueoflegends.com/news/tags/patch-notes/ and https://support-leagueoflegends.riotgames.com/
