# VALORANT core knowledge

- Roles: Duelist, Initiator, Controller, and Sentinel; team needs depend on map and plan, not just role labels.
- Route advice into mechanics, crosshair placement, movement, gunfights, utility, economy, map control, attack, defense, or communication.
- Review plans should prioritize repeatable errors such as first deaths, unused utility, poor trades, forced buys, and predictable positioning.
- Agent abilities, maps, weapon values, rank systems, and competitive map pools are patch-sensitive.
- Current-source starting points: https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/ and https://support-valorant.riotgames.com/
