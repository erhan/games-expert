# Fortnite core knowledge

- First classify the request: Battle Royale, Zero Build, Reload, Creative/UEFN, LEGO Fortnite, Festival, Rocket Racing, or competitive.
- For loadouts, cover range balance, healing, mobility, ammo economy, and mode-specific utility rather than naming items without context.
- For improvement advice, separate landing, early-game looting, rotations, fights, endgame positioning, aim, and replay review.
- Chapters, seasons, quests, item availability, and weapon statistics are highly time-sensitive.
- Current-source starting points: https://www.fortnite.com/news and https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/fortnite
