# Roblox core knowledge

- First split gameplay in a named experience from Roblox Studio development, account help, marketplace questions, and platform safety.
- Experience-specific codes, items, maps, and metas change independently and require experience-specific sources.
- Development answers should account for client/server boundaries, RemoteEvents and RemoteFunctions, DataStore reliability, replication, permissions, and mobile performance.
- Never assume mechanics from one Roblox experience apply to another.
- Current-source starting points: https://create.roblox.com/docs and https://en.help.roblox.com/
