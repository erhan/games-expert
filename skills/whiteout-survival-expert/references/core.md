# Whiteout Survival core knowledge

## Decision context

- State age and hero generation strongly affect available heroes, events, buildings, pets, and upgrade systems.
- Ask for Furnace or Fire Crystal level, hero roster and star levels, troop tiers, Chief Gear/Charm status, alliance role, and spending style only when needed.
- Distinguish Exploration/Arena combat from Expedition, world-map, rally, garrison, and bear-hunt contexts. Skills and formation priorities are not identical.
- Distinguish rally leader recommendations from rally joiner recommendations. The lead hero and the skills contributed by joiners can change the correct formation.

## Common request routes

- Progression: Furnace, Fire Crystal, buildings, research, troops, healing capacity, and resource bottlenecks.
- Heroes: generation availability, shards, stars, widgets or exclusive gear, Hero Gear, formations, and replacement timing.
- Chief development: Chief Gear, Charms, pets, VIP, and scarce-material planning.
- Alliance and combat: rallies, garrisons, Bear Hunt, Crazy Joe, Foundry, Canyon Clash, facilities, fortresses, Sunfire Castle, and SvS.
- Events: preparation, scoring efficiency, saved resources, milestone versus leaderboard goals, and event-specific timing.
- State systems: state age, presidency, ministries, State Transfer, and alliance coordination.

## Reliability rules

- Exact costs, hero availability, event rules, generation timing, gift codes, transfer brackets, and optimal current formations are time-sensitive.
- Confirm the user's state context before recommending heavy investment in hero shards, widgets, Mythic gear, Fire Crystals, Essence Stones, Charm materials, or speedups.
- Prefer official Century Games support and update pages for mechanics and changes. Community guides can fill strategy gaps but should be identified as community guidance.
- Official starting points: https://centurygames.helpshift.com/hc/en/64-whiteout-survival/ and https://www.centurygames.com/
- Useful secondary reference for strategy coverage: https://wiki.wosforge.org/wiki/Whiteout_Survival
