# GTA V and GTA Online core knowledge

- Story Mode and Online have different missions, economies, vehicles, properties, and update histories.
- Route Online questions into beginner progression, businesses, heists, vehicles, PvE/PvP, collectibles, or technical troubleshooting.
- For progression plans, account for starting cash, owned properties, preferred solo/group play, and time available.
- Payouts, bonuses, discounts, event weeks, and platform feature availability are time-sensitive.
- Current-source starting points: https://www.rockstargames.com/newswire and https://support.rockstargames.com/
